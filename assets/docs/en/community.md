# Community Guidelines — Kimi Game Rank

> Official text of the "Community Guidelines" page.
> This site is an interest leaderboard put together by ordinary people who like building things. To keep it **friendly, genuine and full of substance**, we agree on the following rules.
> These guidelines cover **what boundaries each post should respect**; your rights and obligations towards this site are in the [Terms of Service](?p=doc&arg=用户协议).

---

## Chapter 1 — General

### Article 1 (Purpose)

This site welcomes **any sincere expression about a work**: praise, and sharp criticism too. What we oppose is never "unpleasant words" but **insincere words** — rumour, attacks, vote padding, and passing someone else's work off as your own.

### Article 2 (Scope)

These guidelines apply to everything you post or submit here and everything you do here, including:

1. Comments, replies and likes on works;
2. Messages in World Chat;
3. What you ask the AI assistant;
4. Feedback, reports, work recommendations and appeal material;
5. Usernames, avatars and profiles;
6. Access to and use of the site itself.

### Article 3 (Effect and relationship)

1. These guidelines form part of the [Terms of Service](?p=doc&arg=用户协议) and apply together with it, the [Privacy Policy](?p=doc&arg=隐私政策) and the site's other rules.
2. Using this site means accepting these guidelines; **if you do not accept them, do not use it**.
3. Matters not covered here are handled reasonably, in line with the law, public decency and the **spirit** of these guidelines.

---

## Chapter 2 — What we uphold together

### Article 4 (Respect original work)

1. When reposting or quoting someone else's content, **credit the source and the author**.
2. If you see a work plagiarised or stolen, report it — but **stick to facts**, do not accuse on a hunch.
3. The point of listing works is **to let good work be seen**. Give the applause to the real author.

### Article 5 (Be civil)

1. **Be hard on the work, not on the person.** You may say "this design is bad", not "the author is stupid".
2. When you disagree, argue against the **idea**, not the **person**.
3. Against someone emotional, the best move is to **ignore them**, not to be harsher.

### Article 6 (Judge reasonably)

1. Scores are calculated by an algorithm (see the [Scoring Standard](?p=doc&arg=评分标准)). You may question the **rules**, but **scores are not adjusted case by case**.
2. If a category or a score looks wrong, explain the **basis and reasoning**, not just "this is unreasonable".
3. "I don't like it" and "you like it" can both stand; neither needs the other to apologise.

### Article 7 (Share and help)

1. Share gameplay, settings, technical approaches and tools — all welcome.
2. Recommend the **hidden gems** you dig up; the Hidden Gems leaderboard exists for exactly that.
3. Help correct others: work information, categories, dead links — all welcome.

### Article 8 (Protect privacy)

1. **Do not publish other people's personal information**: real names, addresses, phone numbers, social accounts, employers.
2. **Keep your own private too**: comments and chat are visible to everyone, so publishing means giving up control.
3. **Doxxing is strictly forbidden**, whether the target is your "enemy" or your "friend".

### Article 9 (Stay genuine)

1. Do not impersonate anyone, including this site's administrators or co-administrators.
2. Do not fabricate an identity, a source, data or a system message.
3. Do not pad likes, comments or visits — those numbers fool nobody and only destroy what a leaderboard means.

### Article 10 (Spread something positive)

1. We are glad to see valuable findings, useful experience and interesting works.
2. Vented negativity, indiscriminate complaining and arguments unrelated to works are discouraged here.

## Chapter 3 — Prohibited content and behaviour

The following are **red lines**. Breaches are handled under Chapter 5 according to severity.

### Article 11 (Endangering national security and social stability)

Prohibited: content that violates the basic principles established by the Constitution, endangers national security, discloses state secrets, subverts state power, undermines national unity, incites ethnic hatred or discrimination, undermines the state's religious policy, promotes cults or feudal superstition, spreads rumours to disturb public order, or disrupts social stability.

### Article 12 (Illegal and harmful information)

Prohibited: obscene, pornographic, vulgar, gambling, violent, homicidal or terror-related content; trading in or directing people to drugs or prohibited goods; information about fraud, pyramid schemes, illegal fundraising, money laundering or other illegal activity, or driving traffic to it.

### Article 13 (Infringing the lawful rights of others)

Prohibited:

1. Insults, abuse, personal attacks, regional or gender discrimination;
2. Defamation, rumour-mongering, malicious slander, public shaming;
3. **Doxxing**, or publishing another person's real name, address, phone number, social accounts or similar;
4. Malicious harassment, repeated pestering, message bombing;
5. Impersonating another person, or this site's administrators or co-administrators.

### Article 14 (Infringing intellectual property)

Prohibited:

1. Copying, stealing or re-uploading another person's work or content;
2. Reposting copyrighted content without permission;
3. Removing or altering another person's rights notices or attribution;
4. Any other act infringing copyright, trademark, patent, reputation, likeness, privacy or similar rights.

> If you are a rights holder and believe information shown here infringes your rights, article 8 of the [Terms of Service](?p=doc&arg=用户协议) gives **the complete notice process and a template**.

### Article 15 (Disrupting community order)

Prohibited:

1. Spam advertising, traffic farming, recruitment, group invites and selling;
2. Flooding, repeated bumping, meaningless content;
3. Flame wars, factional attacks and agitation unrelated to a work;
4. Malicious spoilers (mark a warning first for plot-critical content);
5. Begging for likes, like-for-like schemes, organised review bombing;
6. Using reports as a tool for revenge or harassment.

### Article 16 (Endangering the platform and technical security)

Prohibited:

1. Automated scripts, crawlers, bulk registration tools;
2. Padding likes, comments or visits;
3. Bypassing rate limits, quotas or other technical protections;
4. Uploading or spreading trojans, viruses or other malicious code;
5. Attacking, scanning, probing, accessing without authorisation or reverse engineering this site or anyone's devices;
6. Scraping, copying or mirroring this site's data for commercial use without permission, or placing an unreasonable load on the service.

### Article 17 (Protection of minors)

1. Do not post content that induces or encourages minors to engage in illegal or dangerous behaviour.
2. Do not post content involving a minor's privacy or infringing a minor's rights.
3. If you are a minor, use this site under the guidance of a guardian and do not disclose personal information.

### Article 18 (Other prohibited cases)

1. Evading an account sanction (for example, opening a new account to continue);
2. Deliberately spreading false information, or faking tool results or system messages;
3. Inducing or encouraging others to do any of the above;
4. Any other act contrary to the law, public decency or the spirit of these guidelines.

> Note: **these guidelines cannot cover every case**. Behaviour that substantially violates the law or public decency may still be handled at our reasonable discretion even if not listed item by item.

## Chapter 4 — Rules for specific areas

### Article 19 (Comment sections)

1. Comments should stay **relevant to the work**.
2. Criticism should say **what exactly is wrong**, not drop a one-star and leave.
3. You can delete your own comment (its replies are removed with it); after deletion it **goes to the recycle bin** and no longer appears.
4. Administrators may **hide** an individual comment (collapsed display) — unlike deletion, the content is not removed and readers can expand it.

### Article 20 (World Chat)

1. World Chat is a **public chat room for the whole site**, visible to everyone; do not post personal information there.
2. Do not flood, argue or advertise in the public channel.
3. Images and Bilibili videos can be sent; messages refresh automatically.
4. You can recall **your own** messages; a recalled message is no longer shown.

### Article 21 (AI assistant)

1. What you ask the AI assistant is subject to these guidelines too.
2. Do not try to make the AI produce prohibited content, or use it for fraud, forgery or infringement.
3. Do not bypass AI quota limits by technical means.
4. AI answers are generated by an algorithm, may be wrong, and **do not represent the views of this site**. See the [AI Guide](?p=doc&arg=AI 使用说明).

### Article 22 (Usernames and avatars)

1. A username is **shown site-wide** and cannot be changed after registration — the only exception is a historical username that does not meet current rules, which is flagged at sign-in for updating. Do not use names that are illegal, infringing, insulting or impersonate officials; usernames may not contain line breaks, carriage returns or decorative special characters (common in harassing garbled text), and they pass content review like any post.
2. Avatars must not be illegal, pornographic, gory or infringing.
3. For a username or avatar that breaks the rules, we will ask for a change or act directly.

### Article 23 (Site emoji)

1. The emoji artwork comes from **Bilibili** and copyright belongs to its rights holders.
2. Emoji are **for on-site conversation only**; do not download them for commercial use or redistribution.
3. This site has **no partnership with or endorsement by Bilibili**. The full statement is in article 8, item 9 of the [Terms of Service](?p=doc&arg=用户协议).

### Article 24 (Blocked words and personal settings)

1. You can add **blocked words** in settings to filter content you do not want to see, **on your own device**.
2. Blocked words affect **only you**; they do not change anyone else's data and are **not a report** — to have content removed, use the report process.
3. You can **turn visit history off** at any time in settings.

## Chapter 5 — Handling violations

### Article 25 (Measures)

Depending on severity we may apply **one or more** of: a reminder, deleting or hiding content, restricting features, muting, banning the account, terminating the service, refusing re-registration.

Apart from reminders and warnings, **account sanctions are published in the site's "Violation notices"** (reason, number of days, current status). A sanctioned user **can still sign in to read that notice**, but site features are unavailable during the sanction.

### Article 26 (Escalation)

| Severity | Typical case | Measure |
|:---:|---|---|
| Minor | First inappropriate remark, mild flooding, messy formatting | Reminder, or delete the content |
| Moderate | Advertising, repeated flooding, personal attacks | Delete content + warning |
| Serious | Infringement, malicious harassment, vote manipulation, malicious spoilers | Mute or restrict features + clean related data |
| Severe | Illegal content, attacking the system, bulk registration, account theft | Ban the account |
| Critical | Suspected criminal offence | Ban + preserve records + report to the authorities as required by law |

### Article 27 (Aggravating and mitigating factors)

**Aggravating**:

1. Deliberately evading a sanction (continuing on a throwaway account);
2. Repeated violations, refusing to change;
3. Organising or encouraging others to violate together;
4. Harming a minor.

**Mitigating**:

1. Removing the offending content voluntarily and undoing its effect;
2. A first, minor violation with a good attitude.

### Article 28 (About "deletion")

1. Most measures are **unlisting / restriction** (no longer shown) rather than physical deletion, so content can be restored if it was a mistake or an appeal succeeds.
2. For content and accounts suspected of illegality or needed for an investigation, we may preserve records and report to the authorities as required by law.

## Chapter 6 — Reports, complaints and appeals

### Article 29 (Reporting)

1. **Where**: the site's "Feedback", optionally marked **visible to administrators only**.
2. **How to make a report effective**: state the work or page, where the offending content is, why it offends, and attach **screenshots or links** where possible. The more specific, the faster.
3. **Malicious reports**: using reports to take revenge on or harass others is handled under Chapter 5.

### Article 30 (Copyright complaints)

If you are a rights holder and need to assert copyright or a similar right, submit a **rights notice** as described in article 8 of the [Terms of Service](?p=doc&arg=用户协议) (with a checklist and a ready-to-use template). We will correct, delete or unlink the material promptly after verification.

### Article 31 (Appeals)

1. If you believe your content was deleted by mistake or your account sanctioned in error, send an appeal through "Feedback" explaining the situation.
2. An appeal **does not suspend the measure** while it is reviewed.
3. We reply with the outcome; if the appeal succeeds, the content or permissions are restored.

### Article 32 (Time limits)

1. General issues are handled as quickly as possible.
2. Rights claims or complex disputes are normally handled and answered **within 15 working days of receipt**.

---

## Chapter 7 — Final provisions

### Article 33 (Updates)

These guidelines may change as the community and the site evolve; changes are published on this page and the date at the bottom is updated. **Continuing to use this site means accepting the updated guidelines.**

### Article 34 (Interpretation and enforcement)

1. The site operator interprets these guidelines, and undertakes to do so **according to the rules and in good faith**.
2. We try to explain the reason for a measure; not explaining does not waive any of your rights.

---

One last thing: these guidelines are not here to "police people", they are here to **reduce misunderstanding**. This site has no large moderation team, and order depends heavily on everyone's own sense of responsibility. Like what is good, report what is not, and argue with reason when you disagree.

Thank you for keeping to these guidelines.
