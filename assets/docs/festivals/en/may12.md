# May 12 Remembrance

## Overview

At 14:28 on 12 May 2008 an earthquake of magnitude 8.0 struck Wenchuan in Sichuan. Every year since, the date has been kept with silence, commemorative gatherings and disaster drills. With the approval of the State Council, 12 May has been the National Disaster Prevention and Reduction Day since 2009.

## Origins

According to the China Earthquake Administration, an earthquake of magnitude 8.0 struck Wenchuan County, Sichuan Province, at 14:28 on 12 May 2008, with its epicentre near Yingxiu and a maximum intensity of XI. As of 12:00 on 18 September 2008, 69,227 people had died, 374,643 were injured and 17,923 were missing. The shock reached Sichuan, Gansu, Shaanxi and neighbouring provinces, with ten counties and cities in the worst-hit category and forty-one in the severely affected one. On 2 March 2009 the State Council approved 12 May as the National Disaster Prevention and Reduction Day, beginning that year.

## Customs

1. **Silence**: three minutes of silence from 14:28, with horns sounded by cars, trains and ships and air-raid sirens raised.
2. **Commemoration**: flowers and remembrance at Wenchuan, the old town of Beichuan and Yingxiu.
3. **Drills**: schools and neighbourhoods run earthquake shelter and evacuation drills, and the publicity week usually falls from 6 to 12 May.
4. **Safety education**: the shelter position, the contents of an emergency kit and evacuation routes.
5. **Visits and volunteering**: returning to rebuilt communities and taking part in public education on disaster preparedness.

## Story

At 14:28 on 12 May 2008 the Wenchuan earthquake struck; the magnitude was first reported as 7.8 and later revised to 8.0. Rescue teams moved into the affected areas from all over the country, and 19 to 21 May were declared national days of mourning, with three minutes of silence observed nationwide at 14:28 on 19 May. Relief work and paired reconstruction assistance continued for several years, and the new county town of Beichuan was completed and occupied in 2010.

## Theme

On a grey-blue ground, one candle and two white chrysanthemums under a thin mist. A fine line starts at the left edge and stops at the middle of the frame, going no further. A line that ends halfway is the measure this day asks for: no pattern stretching outward, no extra colour, only room to be silent for a while.
