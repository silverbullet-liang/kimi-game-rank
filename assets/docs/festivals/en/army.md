# Army Day

## Overview

1 August is the anniversary of the founding of the Chinese People's Liberation Army. The Nanchang Uprising broke out on 1 August 1927, and the date was later fixed as the army's founding anniversary. It is not a statutory holiday. Units hold flag-raising, oath-taking and open days and study army history, while local authorities visit service families and veterans in the same period.

## Origins

On 1 August 1927 the Communist Party of China launched an armed uprising at Nanchang in Jiangxi, and the troops marched south afterwards. On 11 July 1933 the Central Provisional Government of the Chinese Soviet Republic decided to make 1 August the anniversary of the founding of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army. After 1949 the date became generally known as Army Day of the Chinese People's Liberation Army. In the same year the Revolutionary Military Commission ordered that the figures "eight one" be used as the mark on the army flag and emblem, so that date and mark were brought together.

## Customs

1. **Flag ceremonies**: barracks raise the national and army flags on 1 August and personnel repeat the service oath.
2. **Parades and open days**: parades are held in some years; in ordinary years barracks open days and equipment displays receive the public.
3. **Support for service families**: before the day, local authorities visit the families of serving personnel and of the fallen, and of veterans, and apply the preferential policies.
4. **Army history**: visits to army museums and revolutionary sites, and talks on tradition.
5. **Concerts and visits**: military and civilian companies perform in barracks, and gift parcels are sent.

## Story

In the early hours of 1 August 1927 the shooting in Nanchang began earlier than planned. Zhou Enlai was secretary of the front committee, and He Long, Ye Ting, Zhu De and Liu Bocheng led units in the action; the force of more than twenty thousand took the city after several hours of fighting. The troops then moved south into Guangdong, and after further marches part of them joined Mao Zedong's forces on Jinggangshan in 1928. In the summer of 1933 the central authorities fixed 1 August as the anniversary of the Red Army's founding, and the first observance was held on 1 August that year. Recollections and archives give accounts of that night that differ in some details.

## Theme

This skin lays a very fine warp-and-weft grid over the whole page, like a length of army-green cloth spread out, with a red star placed in the top right corner. The grid is not a regular printed screen but a weave with slight rises and falls, so that the direction of the cloth shows where light falls on it; the star is the only saturated colour on the page, its edge pressed onto the weave and never touching the margin. There is no button and no seam anywhere.
