# Teachers' Day

## Overview

10 September is Teachers' Day in China, established in 1985. It is not a statutory holiday, and schools mark it with award ceremonies, flowers and special activities. For comparison, Taiwan keeps Teachers' Day on 28 September, the reported birthday of Confucius, and UNESCO has held World Teachers' Day on 5 October since 1994.

## Origins

Respect for teachers is an old theme in China, and the birthday of Confucius was traditionally the day of sacrifices to him, with academies and village schools holding ceremonies at the start of term and at the year's end. The modern date changed several times. In 1931 Tai Shuangqiu, Cheng Qibao and others proposed 6 June as Teachers' Day and issued a declaration calling for better pay, security of post and improved training. In 1939 the government of the time moved the day to the birthday of Confucius. After 1949, a 1951 announcement made 1 May, International Labour Day, serve as Teachers' Day as well. On 21 January 1985 the Standing Committee of the 6th National People's Congress adopted its decision on establishing Teachers' Day, approving the State Council's proposal and fixing 10 September each year.

## Customs

1. **Awards and visits**: schools and education departments select outstanding teachers before the day and call on retired and village teachers.
2. **Flowers and cards**: students make cards, write letters of thanks, or stand together to greet the teacher before class.
3. **Class meetings and professional ethics**: sessions on the theme of respecting teachers, and new teachers take their oath on the day.
4. **Open classes**: some schools turn the day into open classrooms and invite parents and fellow teachers to sit in.
5. **Public attention**: media run stories about teachers, and some scenic sites admit teachers free on the day.

## Story

Tradition gives Confucius three thousand disciples, and the Records of the Grand Historian, in its chapter on the disciples, says that seventy-seven of them mastered what he taught. Village schools used to bow before his image at the start of term and at festivals. In December 1984 several professors at Beijing Normal University put their names to a proposal for a teachers' day, and on 21 January of the following year the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress adopted the decision. One reason for 10 September was timing: the school year has just begun and activities are easy to arrange, and few national observances fall in September, so a campaign would not crowd another date. The first Teachers' Day was 10 September 1985.

## Theme

This skin is a length of chalk and three specks of chalk dust on a blackboard green. The chalk lies at an angle, one end already worn round, and the three specks are scattered beside it, of different sizes and in no regular arrangement. The green is the green of a board wiped over many times, uneven in depth; there is only one white, the white of chalk. No words are written on the page and no erasure is shown, only what is left after writing.
