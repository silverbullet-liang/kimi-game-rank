# International Children's Day

## Overview

Observed on 1 June and known in Chinese as the June First International Children's Day. In November 1949 the Women's International Democratic Federation held a council meeting in Moscow and designated 1 June as International Children's Day; on 23 December of the same year the Government Administration Council of the Central People's Government ruled that China's children's day should fall on the same date.

## Origins

The day is linked to the Lidice tragedy of 1942. On 10 June 1942 Nazi German forces destroyed the village of Lidice in Czechoslovakia, killing the adult men and sending the women and children to camps. In November 1949 the Women's International Democratic Federation met in Moscow and, to mourn the children of Lidice and of the war and to oppose the killing and poisoning of children, designated 1 June as International Children's Day, with aims that include the right to survival, to health care and to education.

## Customs

1. **Performances**: schools and kindergartens stage shows in which children sing, dance and act.
2. **Pioneer ceremonies**: primary schools hold induction ceremonies for the Young Pioneers around the date.
3. **Gifts and fairs**: parents give books, stationery and toys, and parks set up games and stalls.
4. **Free entry**: libraries, science centres and zoos admit children free or at a reduced rate.
5. **Care visits**: trips to welfare homes and school supplies for children in difficulty.

## Story

In May 1942 Reinhard Heydrich, the Nazi German governor of Bohemia and Moravia, was assassinated. On 10 June German forces surrounded Lidice outside Prague, shot the men over fifteen, sent the women and children to camps and burned the village. Post-war enquiries found that most of the village children did not survive. In November 1949, at its council meeting in Moscow, the Women's International Democratic Federation joined that memory to children's rights to survival, health care and education and resolved to establish International Children's Day; in the same month the Chinese Government Administration Council issued a corresponding ruling.

## Theme

Three building blocks stacked out of line, each a different colour, and in the upper right a paper plane flying diagonally upward. Blocks are made to be built up and knocked down; a paper plane is folded to be thrown away. Both are in the picture and both say the same thing: whatever is at hand is what you play with first.
