# World Environment Day

## Overview

Observed on 5 June and established by the United Nations. The UN Conference on the Human Environment met in Stockholm from 5 to 16 June 1972, and in October that year the 27th session of the General Assembly set 5 June, the opening day of the conference, as World Environment Day. The UN Environment Programme announces a theme each year and holds commemorative events.

## Origins

The Stockholm conference was the first UN meeting to take up environmental questions on a global scale. It raised the slogan Only One Earth and adopted the Declaration of the United Nations Conference on the Human Environment, with an action plan of 109 recommendations. In October 1972 the 27th session of the General Assembly fixed 5 June as World Environment Day, and in January 1973 the General Assembly established the UN Environment Programme, which sets the annual theme. China has held publicity events on 5 June since the 1980s, and the Environmental Protection Law in force since 2015 writes the date into law.

## Customs

1. **Theme announcement**: the environment programme names a theme and a host city each year, and countries organise around it.
2. **Planting and clean-ups**: riverbank clearing, tree planting and campaigns on sorting waste.
3. **Company action**: emission pledges, greener supply chains, open days showing cleaner processes.
4. **Environmental education**: outdoor classes, craft from used materials, low-carbon checklists.
5. **Exhibitions and screenings**: photography shows and documentaries on water, air and biodiversity.

## Story

On 5 June 1972 the Stockholm conference opened with more than 1,300 delegates from 113 countries. In preparation the secretary-general Maurice Strong commissioned experts from several dozen countries to write an informal report entitled Only One Earth, which became the basis of discussion. The conference adopted the Declaration on the Human Environment with seven common views and twenty-six principles, and an action plan with 109 recommendations. It proposed that its opening day be kept as World Environment Day, which the 27th session of the General Assembly approved in October that year.

## Theme

A whole leaf with its veins drawn in fine lines, branching from the midrib to either side and running out to the edge. Beyond the leaf there is no stem and no background, and the green stays in the lines. The veins are a tree in miniature: water and nutrients divide from one line into every finer one, and the leaf lives by that distribution.
