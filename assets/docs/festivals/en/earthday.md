# Earth Day

## Overview

Earth Day is observed on 22 April. In Chinese usage the day is called World Earth Day, and in 2009 the 63rd session of the United Nations General Assembly designated 22 April as the International Mother Earth Day. Lectures, clean-ups, tree planting and public campaigns are held around the world on the date, and China has run official publicity events each year since the 1990s, with a theme chosen annually.

## Origins

On 22 April 1970 the United States senator Gaylord Nelson and the Harvard law student Denis Hayes organised a nationwide environmental programme in which some twenty million people took part; it is regarded as the beginning of the modern environmental movement. On the twentieth anniversary in 1990 the event spread to more than 140 countries, and from then on it became a global occasion. In 2009 the 63rd session of the UN General Assembly adopted a resolution naming 22 April the International Mother Earth Day.

## Customs

1. **Annual theme**: organisers announce a theme each year, and exhibitions, lectures and school activities are built around it.
2. **Clean-ups and planting**: neighbourhood groups clear riverbanks, pick up litter, plant trees and adopt green spaces.
3. **Low-carbon travel**: walking, cycling and public transport are encouraged, and offices try paperless routines.
4. **Nature watching**: bird counts, plant identification and seasonal records, learning by looking rather than by lecture.
5. **Swapping and mending**: second-hand markets and clothing repairs that keep objects in use longer.

## Story

In January 1969 an offshore well near Santa Barbara, California, blew out. Crude oil covered the sea, great numbers of seabirds and marine animals died, and the footage reached households across the country. By several accounts, Senator Gaylord Nelson flew over the spill, came away with the idea of a nationwide environmental event, floated it in campus speeches, and left the organising to Denis Hayes. On 22 April 1970 about twenty million people joined from ten thousand schools, two thousand colleges and two thousand communities, and the date came to be called the first Earth Day.

## Theme

A sphere drawn only by its rim, with a band of cloud crossing it and blue meeting green along the curve. Nothing is painted inside; only the outline remains, the way the Earth looks from far away, where mountains and cities flatten into one thin line. The cloud band lies on that line, with blue and green trading places, sea and land as two sides of the same surface.
