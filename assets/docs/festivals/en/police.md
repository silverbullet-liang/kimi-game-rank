# Chinese People's Police Day

## Overview

Observed on 10 January each year, Chinese People's Police Day (中国人民警察节) is a national-level day for the people's police. The date is taken from 110, the police emergency number. It was approved by the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and confirmed by the State Council in 2020, and has been observed since 2021.

## Origins

On 10 January 1986 the Guangzhou Municipal Public Security Bureau set up China's first 110 emergency call centre. In 1996 the Ministry of Public Security held a national meeting in Zhangzhou, Fujian, to spread the "Zhangzhou 110" model, after which 110 became the force's best-known public number. In July 2020 the State Council issued its reply approving the establishment of the day (Guohan [2020] No. 98): from 2021 onward, 10 January is Chinese People's Police Day, with the Ministry of Public Security organising activities together with relevant departments.

## Customs

1. **Flag and oath**: public security organs hold flag-raising ceremonies and re-read the police oath around this date.
2. **Open police stations**: some stations, police boxes and traffic units open to the public, showing equipment and explaining how calls are handled.
3. **110 publicity**: because 1-1-0 matches the date, the day is used to explain how and when to call 110 and to discourage misuse of emergency lines.
4. **Honours**: medals for long service, and awards for outstanding units and individuals.
5. **Visits**: officers visit the families of colleagues killed or injured on duty, and retired officers.

## Story

The date comes out of institutional history. In the mid-1980s, growing crime reports and requests for help in coastal cities overwhelmed the old reporting channels, so Guangzhou's police opened China's first 110 emergency call centre on 10 January 1986, gathering burglary, robbery and general help calls under one three-digit number. After the 1996 Zhangzhou meeting the number spread to cities nationwide and widened from crime reports to requests for help. More than three decades later it was chosen as the date of the day, for a plain reason: this is where the number began, and it is the number most people know.

## Theme

The skin works in deep blue with silver-grey lines: at the upper right an octagonal emblem is drawn as outline only, and two diagonal cordon tapes cross the left side. The octagon follows the outer shape of the police badge; the tapes follow the boundary cordoned off at a scene. No figures, no equipment — only an emblem and a line, which are the two things this day is about: the identity of a service, and the order it keeps.
