# Anniversary of the Founding of the Communist Party of China

## Overview

1 July is the anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China, commonly called Party Day. The Party was founded in 1921, and the observance is held on 1 July. It is not a statutory holiday; in the days around it, organisations at various levels hold commemorative meetings, award ceremonies and oath-taking for new members.

## Origins

In July 1921 the First National Congress of the Communist Party of China opened in Shanghai, and its final session was held on a boat on South Lake at Jiaxing in Zhejiang, where the founding of the Party was proclaimed. The actual opening date of the congress was later established by research as 23 July. The use of 1 July as the anniversary began during the War of Resistance: in May 1938 Mao Zedong, writing On Protracted War, named 1 July as the anniversary of the Party's founding, and in June 1941 the Central Committee issued a directive on marking the twentieth anniversary of the founding, which fixed 1 July from then on.

## Customs

1. **Commemorative meetings**: organisations at each level hold meetings, forums and reports around 1 July.
2. **Awards**: advanced grassroots organisations, outstanding members and party affairs workers are commended.
3. **Oath-taking**: new members read the admission oath before the Party flag.
4. **Visits and themed days**: trips to revolutionary sites, memorial halls and special exhibitions.
5. **Party classes and study**: lectures on Party history, quizzes and reading activities.

## Story

In May 1938, in a cave dwelling at Yan'an, Mao Zedong wrote in On Protracted War that "1 July this year is the seventeenth anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China". Seventeen years had passed since the July 1921 congress, and in wartime conditions the exact date of the meeting was not easy to check, so July became the received account. In June 1941 the Central Committee issued its directive on the twentieth anniversary of the founding and the fourth anniversary of the war of resistance, the document that made 1 July the official anniversary. The opening date of 23 July was arrived at later, by research into archives and the recollections of those present.
