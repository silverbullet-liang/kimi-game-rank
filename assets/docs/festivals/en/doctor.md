# Chinese Doctors' Day

## Overview

19 August is Chinese Doctors' Day. On 3 November 2017 the State Council approved a request to make 19 August each year Chinese Doctors' Day from 2018 onwards, with the former National Health and Family Planning Commission, now the National Health Commission, organising the observance with the relevant departments. 19 August 2018 was the first such day. It is not a statutory holiday, and hospitals and health bodies mark it with award ceremonies, free clinics and open days.

## Origins

The choice of date goes back to 19 August 2016, when the National Conference on Health was held in Beijing. Proposals for a doctors' day had been put forward for years by professional bodies, delegates to the people's congresses and members of the political consultative conferences. Chinese Doctors' Day is the fourth professional day established in China, after Nurses' Day, Teachers' Day and Journalists' Day.

## Customs

1. **Awards**: health authorities at each level and individual hospitals select outstanding doctors and teams and hold ceremonies before the day.
2. **Free clinics and public talks**: hospitals send specialists to communities and villages for consultations, health lectures and first aid training.
3. **Open days**: some hospitals open operating theatres, laboratories and teaching areas so the public can see how diagnosis and treatment work.
4. **Visits**: retired doctors, village doctors and the families of medical teams serving abroad or in Xinjiang and Tibet are visited.
5. **Oaths**: new doctors read the physicians' oath, and senior colleagues repeat it on the day.

## Story

On 19 August 2016 the National Conference on Health opened in Beijing. More than a year later, on 3 November 2017, the State Council approved the request from the former National Health and Family Planning Commission to set 19 August as Chinese Doctors' Day from 2018. On 19 August 2018, the first such day, hospitals around the country held short ceremonies, and some departments kept their ordinary rosters. The date answers to the day of that conference. Nurses' Day, by contrast, keeps 12 May, Nightingale's birthday, so the two professional days come from different sources.
