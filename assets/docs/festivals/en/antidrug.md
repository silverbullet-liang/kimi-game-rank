# International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking

## Overview

26 June is the International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking, also called World Drug Day. The United Nations General Assembly established it to support action and cooperation against drug abuse and trafficking. Each year the UN Office on Drugs and Crime sets a theme, and governments, communities and schools hold campaigns around the date.

## Origins

From 12 to 26 June 1987 the United Nations held an International Conference on Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking in Vienna, with delegates from 138 countries, and the conference recommended an international day. On 7 December 1987 the General Assembly adopted resolution 42/112, designating 26 June as the International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking. The date matches the closing day of the Vienna conference. Themes have been attached to the observance since 1992. The first international conference on narcotics had met in Shanghai in 1909, and three drug control conventions were adopted in 1961, 1971 and 1988.

## Customs

1. **Themed campaigns**: UNODC issues a theme and the World Drug Report each year, and local events are built around them.
2. **School education**: lectures, exhibitions and class sessions on the health risks of drug use.
3. **Community advice and treatment services**: treatment centres hold open days and give out information on treatment, rehabilitation and family support.
4. **Seizures and enforcement briefings**: some countries publish interception figures or destroy confiscated drugs on this date.
5. **China's campaigns**: around 26 June, governments at each level run concentrated publicity through newspapers, broadcasting, television and online. The Anti-Drug Law of the People's Republic of China was adopted in December 2007 and took effect on 1 June 2008.

## Story

A background fragment connected with this date. In the conference hall in Vienna in 1987 the delegates were dealing with trafficking routes, the movement of precursor chemicals and the gaps in treatment for people who are dependent. The meeting ran fifteen days, and the report it produced proposed that countries set aside one day a year to explain the matter. Half a year later the resolution passed in New York. For more than thirty years since, the World Drug Report issued around this date has recorded global use, seizure volumes and the number of new psychoactive substances appearing each year. The observance itself is simple: put a set of figures in front of the public.

## Theme

This skin draws a broken chain: deep blue and silver, three whole links, and then a fourth that slips out of alignment, no longer joined. The first three still hold; the last one is only slightly off before it comes free, and the break is left as empty space, with no sparks and no shattering. A chain means binding and also continuity, and the link that has come loose is the one place where the chain can stop.
