# Long March Victory Day

## Overview

22 October. On this day in 1936 the Second Front Army and the First Front Army met at Jiangtaibao in Gansu; with the three main forces then fully joined, the Long March — two years in the making — came to an end. The date is commonly observed as the anniversary of that victory, and some accounts treat the whole of October as the commemorative period. This site calculates the number of years automatically.

## Origins

In October 1934 the Central Red Army set out from Yudu in Jiangxi on a strategic transfer. After the Zunyi Conference in January 1935 it crossed the Chishui River four times and slipped across the Jinsha River, forced the Dadu River and took Luding Bridge in May, crossed the Jiajin mountains and other snow-covered ranges in June, and traversed the Songpan grasslands in August. In October 1935 the Central Red Army reached Wuqi Town in northern Shaanxi. In October 1936 the Second and Fourth Front Armies joined the First Front Army in the Huining and Jiangtaibao area of Gansu, and the Long March ended there. The Central Red Army's route ran roughly 25,000 li and passed through more than ten provinces. The meeting at Jiangtaibao on 22 October 1936 marked the joining of the three main forces, and the date has been observed as the anniversary ever since.

## Customs

1. **Commemorative events**: assemblies, themed exhibitions and academic seminars are held, and museums along the route open special displays around this date.
2. **Walking and study programmes**: schools and groups organise hikes and field study along sections of the route, most often in autumn.
3. **Visiting the sites**: the old sites and memorial halls at Yudu, Zunyi, Huining and Jiangtaibao see concentrated visits and wreath-laying on the day.
4. **Documents and film**: archives release related records, and books and films are published around this period.

## Story

In May 1935, after forcing the Dadu River at Anshunchang, the main force had to take Luding Bridge quickly. The advance units marched 240 li in a day and a night to reach the western end, where the planks had been removed and only thirteen iron chains spanned the torrent. Twenty-two shock troops, submachine guns in hand and sabres on their backs, crawled forward along the chains while the following units laid planks behind them, and the eastern bank was taken. In the same period the army crossed snow mountains such as Jiajin, over 4,000 metres high with thin air and permanent snow, and the swamps of the Songpan grasslands, where many soldiers did not come out. These place names and figures later became the most frequently cited passages in accounts of the march.

## Theme

This skin draws a range of snow mountains and a road heading north: the mountains are outlines only, a very pale cold white pressing along the ridge line, and a diagonal band widening from the bottom of the frame towards the distance stands for the column moving north. A single dark red note appears once; there is no festive colour anywhere, and drifting snow is the only motion on the page.
