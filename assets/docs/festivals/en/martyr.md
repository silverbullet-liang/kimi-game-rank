# Martyrs' Day

## Overview

On 31 August 2014 the Standing Committee of the Twelfth National People's Congress adopted the Decision on Establishing a Martyrs' Day, giving legal form to 30 September as the date and providing that the state holds commemorative activities on it every year. The first observance was on 30 September 2014. On 27 April 2018 the Law on the Protection of Heroes and Martyrs was adopted by the Standing Committee of the Thirteenth National People's Congress.

## Origins

The date is tied to the Monument to the People's Heroes. On 30 September 1949 the First Plenary Session of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference resolved to build the monument in Tiananmen Square, and the foundation stone was laid that afternoon. Holding the commemoration on the day before National Day joins it to the holiday that follows and to the wreath-laying at the monument on the morning of 1 October. According to incomplete figures, some twenty million people have died for national independence, liberation and the building of the country since the modern period; because of wartime conditions, only a minority of them left names that are recorded in the martyrs' registers kept at various levels.

## Customs

1. **Wreaths at the Monument to the People's Heroes**: held on the morning of 30 September in Tiananmen Square, attended by state leaders and representatives from the capital; a military band plays the national anthem, the square falls silent, and honour guards carry nine large baskets forward on ribbons reading "The People's Heroes Are Immortal".
2. **Silence and viewing**: after the ceremony participants walk around the monument, and members of the public come forward with flowers.
3. **Graves and memorial sites**: martyrs' cemeteries, graves and memorial halls across the country hold observances in the days around the date.
4. **Finding names**: in recent years a practice has grown of tracing the names and families of martyrs who were unidentified or whose relatives lost contact.
5. **Online commemoration**: virtual flowers and messages, and themed lessons in primary and secondary schools.

## Story

The Monument to the People's Heroes had its foundation stone laid on 30 September 1949, was begun in 1952 and completed and unveiled in 1958; it stands 37.94 metres high, and the stone for its front face came from Mount Fu in Qingdao. The inscription was drafted by Mao Zedong and written out by Zhou Enlai. It has three parts, opening with "the past three years", moving to "the past thirty years", and ending with a turn back to 1840, so that the dead of different periods are placed inside one stone. It is said that Zhou practised the characters for days before writing them. Eight characters face the square; the three passages are on the back; nothing else is added.

## Theme

In the upper right there is a single small star, unlit and not enlarged. The weight of the picture sits in the row below; the star is kept for one person only.
